Project Overview

A complete brand identity for Rashfa Cafe, featuring a custom logo that creatively combines the letter 'R' with a coffee cup silhouette. The project included developing a full brand system — color palette, typography, business cards, menu designs, packaging, and social media templates — all designed to create a warm, inviting atmosphere that reflects the cafe's personality.
